Modal component

Vanilla JS, no dependencies. Opens on click, closes on the X button, a backdrop click, or Escape. Focus is trapped inside while open and restored to the trigger on close.

About this component

This dialog follows the WAI-ARIA Dialog (Modal) pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", and aria-labelledby pointing at its heading.

Try tabbing through the controls below — focus should never leave the dialog while it's open, and Shift+Tab from the first element wraps to the last.
